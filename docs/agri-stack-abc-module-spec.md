## 0. Context for Claude Code

Ethiopia's **Agribusiness Company (ABC) Proclamation** was ratified on June 26, 2026. It lets smallholders, pastoralists and agropastoralists form farmer-owned companies, contributing **land-use rights, livestock and labour as capital**. Cooperatives and cluster farming groups may convert into ABCs. ATI launched the first ABC (**Jiru**, Moretna Jiru, North Shewa, Amhara) on July 26, 2026, with **50 planned** nationwide. About **9.5 million cluster farmers** may convert over time.

ABCs may: supply inputs (seed, fertilizer, agrochemicals, machinery, livestock), process products, produce and market jointly, access loans, foreign exchange and insurance, and export.

**Draft-stage rules (as reported; the final law and ministerial directives may differ):**
- Minimum founding members: **300** (crop/grain), **150** (horticulture, fruit, livestock); adjustable by directive
- Share value ≥ **1,000 ETB**, all shares equal; minimum shares per member: **5** (grain), **10** (horticulture/livestock)
- Minimum paid-up capital **1.5M ETB**; registration once ≥25% of declared capital is paid
- Extra pledged shares: ≥25% upfront, the rest within **2 years**
- No member may hold more than **15%** of shares offered
- **Voting weighted by registered land and production volume**
- Organizing committee of **5–13** members, recognized by the Ministry or region
- Registration needs a founding agreement, board names and signatures, a **3–5 year business plan**, and proof of deposited capital
- Valuation of in-kind contributions is set by **ministerial directive** (may not be issued yet)
- Incentives: tax-free import of machinery and inputs; priority foreign currency; members' sales and dividends income-tax exempt; company pays corporate tax

### Hard rules for implementation
1. **Every legal number above is a configurable rule**, stored in an admin-editable "ABC Rules" table with effective dates and source reference. **Never hard-code them.**
2. The platform keeps **support records**, not legal documents. Every share certificate, register export or minutes export carries: *"Generated by Agri-Stack 360. Official record is held by the ABC and the registering authority."*
3. In-kind valuation (land, livestock, labour) uses a pluggable **valuation method** that admins configure once the directive exists. Until then: manual entry by the ABC with a "valuation pending directive" flag.
4. Reuse the platform's existing patterns: server-side permission checks, 4 languages (English, Amharic, Afaan Oromoo, Tigrinya), demo-data labels, audit log, tests.
5. Personal data stays in the platform. Never send names, phones or IDs to external AI APIs. Consent is recorded for every member.
6. **Neutral advice:** if the ABC sells inputs to members, advisory still recommends by active ingredient and lists all approved suppliers. The ABC shop is labelled, never ranked first by default.

## 1. Roles inside an ABC

| Role | Who | Main permissions |
|---|---|---|
| **Organizing Committee member** | 5–13 founders before registration | Formation workspace, member enrolment, draft documents |
| **General Manager** | Hired manager | Full operations: members, inputs, sales, finance, reports |
| **Board member / Chair** | Elected by general assembly | View everything, approve purchases and sales above limits, call meetings, sign approvals |
| **Accountant / Finance officer** | ABC staff | Share payments, ledger, member accounts, dividends, invoices |
| **Store / Warehouse keeper** | ABC staff | Stock in/out, deliveries, produce intake, quality grading |
| **Machinery coordinator / Operator** | ABC staff | Machinery schedule, job logs, fuel and maintenance |
| **Field agronomist / ABC extension officer** | ABC staff or DA | Member advisory, diagnoses, production plans |
| **Member (shareholder farmer)** | Farmer | Own shares, plots, orders, deliveries, payments, votes, advisory |
| **Auditor** | Internal or external | Read-only finance and governance records |
| **Woreda / Regional officer** | Government | Read-only: ABC status, compliance, aggregated reports |
| **ATI ABC program officer** | ATI | Read-only portfolio dashboard across all ABCs |

Permissions are checked on the server for every request. A user can hold roles in more than one ABC, for example a DA supporting several.

## 2. Features (checklist)

### 2.1 Formation & registration
- [ ] Create an ABC in **formation** status: name, type (crop/grain, horticulture, fruit, livestock, mixed), region/zone/woreda/kebele (official codes)
- [ ] Organizing committee: add 5–13 members, record recognition by Ministry or region (document upload + date)
- [ ] **Readiness checklist** driven by the rules table: members ≥ minimum, paid capital ≥ minimum, ≥25% of declared capital paid, board elected, business plan attached, capital deposit proof attached
- [ ] Founding agreement / articles: template in 4 languages, member signature tracking (digital confirmation or uploaded signed scan)
- [ ] **Business plan builder (3–5 years):** pulls members' plots, crops, expected yields, input needs, machinery and price forecasts; exports PDF; editable narrative
- [ ] Registration record: authority, registration number, date, certificate upload; status changes to **registered**
- [ ] Cooperative or cluster **conversion** path: import existing member list and assets, then map them to shares

### 2.2 Member & shareholder registry
- [ ] Enrol member: link to existing farmer profile or create one; national ID / Fayda reference **stored securely, masked in UI**; photo; gender and age group (for inclusion reporting); consent record
- [ ] Bulk import of members (CSV/Excel) with validation and duplicate detection
- [ ] **Contributions** per member:
  - Cash: shares bought, amount, payment schedule
  - **Land-use rights:** plots (area ha, location, land certificate reference, soil, irrigation), contribution status
  - **Livestock:** type, count, valuation
  - **Labour:** days or role, valuation
  - Valuation method + "pending directive" flag
- [ ] **Share ledger:** shares pledged, paid, outstanding; certificate numbers; transfers between members (with board approval); share history
- [ ] **Rule checks:** minimum shares per member type, **15% ownership cap**, full payment of minimum shares, **2-year deadline** for pledged extra shares with automatic reminders
- [ ] **Voting weight calculator:** based on registered land and production volume (formula configurable); shows each member's weight and the total; recalculated each season
- [ ] Member statement: shares, payments, deliveries, purchases, balance, dividends (printable, 4 languages, readable aloud)
- [ ] Member exit, death or inheritance handling: transfer shares to heirs or other members with approvals and an audit trail

### 2.3 Governance
- [ ] Board and officers: terms, roles, election records
- [ ] **General assembly & board meetings:** schedule, agenda, notice by SMS/Telegram, attendance, **quorum check using voting weights**
- [ ] **Voting:** motions, weighted votes, results; in-person entry by the secretary, with optional member confirmation by phone
- [ ] Minutes and resolutions: draft, approve, archive, export PDF
- [ ] **Approval workflows:** purchases and sales above board-set limits need board approval; full audit trail
- [ ] Policy documents library: by-laws, pricing policy, quality standards

### 2.4 Finance & accounting
- [ ] Share capital account and payment collection: cash, bank, mobile money (telebirr / M-Pesa reference recorded)
- [ ] Simple **ledger:** income, expenses, assets (tractors, stores), liabilities, loans
- [ ] **Member accounts:** input credit given, deductions from produce payments, balances
- [ ] Invoices and receipts (to members and buyers), in 4 languages
- [ ] Budget vs actual against the business plan
- [ ] **Profit & dividend distribution:** profit for the year, reserves set by policy, dividend per share, member payout list, payment records
- [ ] Tax support: track exemption-eligible imports (machinery, inputs, construction materials) with documents; corporate tax period reports. **Not tax advice;** exports for the accountant
- [ ] Export to Excel/PDF for auditors and banks

### 2.5 Production planning & advisory (uses existing Agri-Stack modules)
- [ ] Season plan: crops, varieties, area per member, planting windows from the **crop calendar**
- [ ] Member advisory: AI assistant, photo diagnosis, **outbreak alerts** to all members in an area
- [ ] **Broadcast** messages to all or selected members (SMS, Telegram, app, voice), in 4 languages
- [ ] Field agronomist task list: visits, diagnoses to verify, follow-ups
- [ ] Yield estimates per plot and in total, feeding marketing and the business plan

### 2.6 Input supply to members
- [ ] **Demand aggregation:** input needs calculated from plots × crop × recommendations; members confirm orders
- [ ] Supplier comparison from **Input Hub** (price, distance, registration status)
- [ ] Purchase orders to suppliers, with board approval above limit; delivery tracking
- [ ] **Input Trust verification** at receipt: scan codes, flag fakes or duplicates, block distribution of unverified stock
- [ ] Stock management: receipts, issues to members, balances, **expiry dates** (warn before expiry)
- [ ] Sales or credit to members: price, quantity, payment or deduction from future produce
- [ ] Only **registered** products can be listed; banned or expired products are blocked

### 2.7 Machinery & services
- [ ] Machinery register (ATI-provided tractors, truck, etc.): ownership, condition, documents
- [ ] **Booking calendar:** members request ploughing, harvesting, transport; priority and route planning by plot location
- [ ] Job log: hectares or hours, operator, fuel; charge to member account
- [ ] Maintenance schedule and costs; spare parts
- [ ] Utilisation dashboard (hours used vs available)

### 2.8 Output aggregation, storage & quality
- [ ] Harvest forecast by crop and week (from plots and calendar)
- [ ] **Produce intake:** member, crop, quantity, **quality grade** (moisture, purity), weighbridge entry; receipt to member via SMS
- [ ] Warehouse stock by crop and grade; losses and shrinkage tracking (post-harvest)
- [ ] Storage advice and pest alerts for stored grain (from knowledge base)
- [ ] Member payment for delivered produce: price, deductions (input credit, services), net pay

### 2.9 Marketing, sales & processing
- [ ] **Market intelligence:** NMIS prices and forecasts, selling-time advice for the ABC
- [ ] Buyer directory (processors, unions, exporters, traders) and offer/negotiation records
- [ ] **Sales contracts:** buyer, crop, grade, quantity, price, delivery schedule, payment terms; board approval
- [ ] Dispatch and delivery notes; buyer invoices and payment tracking
- [ ] Processing (optional): batches, inputs, outputs, yield, costs
- [ ] **Traceability:** lot → contributing members → plots → inputs used; export certificate support documents
- [ ] Export readiness checklist (documents only, no legal advice)

### 2.10 Finance & insurance access (Phase 2)
- [ ] **Loan application pack:** business plan, financials, production history, member data summary (with member consent)
- [ ] Loan tracking: lender, amount, repayment schedule, reminders
- [ ] Crop insurance enrolment records and claims support (yield/weather evidence)
- [ ] Consent-based member credit profiles for partner MFIs

### 2.11 Compliance & reporting
- [ ] **Compliance dashboard** driven by the rules table: member minimum, capital, share deadlines, ownership cap, meeting frequency, annual report due
- [ ] Periodic reports for the registering authority, woreda and ATI (members, capital, production, sales, profit, inclusion by gender and age group)
- [ ] Annual report generator (PDF, 4 languages)
- [ ] Document vault: registration, agreements, minutes, contracts, audits, with version history
- [ ] **Audit log** of all financial, share and governance changes (who, when, before/after)

### 2.12 ATI & government portfolio view (read-only)
- [ ] ATI program dashboard across all ABCs: formation status, members, capital mobilized, machinery utilisation, production, sales, compliance alerts
- [ ] Map of ABCs by region/zone/woreda
- [ ] Comparison and benchmarking between ABCs
- [ ] Exports; **no individual member identities** unless the role permits

### 2.13 Member experience (farmer side)
- [ ] "My ABC" screen: my shares and certificates, my voting weight, payments due, my plots, my orders, my deliveries and payments, dividends
- [ ] Meeting notices and voting confirmations
- [ ] Machinery booking and status
- [ ] Works on **basic phones**: SMS for receipts, payment confirmations, meeting notices; USSD or IVR later
- [ ] Read-aloud for statements and notices

### 2.14 Cross-cutting
- [ ] Multi-tenant: each ABC sees only its own data
- [ ] Offline-capable data entry for store keepers and field staff, syncing later
- [ ] All screens and documents in 4 languages; amounts in ETB; Ethiopian and Gregorian calendar dates
- [ ] Backup and restore per ABC; data export on request
- [ ] Subscription and billing for the ABC (15,000 ETB/year, configurable)

## 4. Acceptance tests (examples)

- Rule change in `abc_rule` (e.g. minimum members 300 → 250) updates readiness checks without code changes.
- A member cannot exceed the ownership cap; the system blocks the share purchase and explains why in the member's language.
- Voting weight totals match the formula on fixture data; quorum check uses weights.
- Unverified or duplicate Input Trust codes block stock issue to members.
- Produce payment = price × quantity − input credit − service charges, matching fixture data.
- Dividend run distributes exactly the approved amount, rounding handled, and each member statement updates.
- Woreda officer cannot see member names or IDs; ATI officer sees only aggregates.
- Every share, finance and governance change creates an audit log entry.

## 6. Phases

### Pilot MVP (for Jiru ABC, about 8–10 weeks)
2.1 formation checklist + registration record · 2.2 member registry, cash shares, plots, share ledger, rule checks, voting weights · 2.3 meetings + weighted votes (basic) · 2.4 share payments + member accounts + simple ledger · 2.5 broadcast + advisory link · 2.6 demand aggregation + Input Trust at receipt + stock · 2.7 machinery booking + job log · 2.12 ATI read-only dashboard · 2.13 "My ABC" + SMS receipts

### Phase 2
In-kind valuation (after the directive), produce intake and quality, member produce payments, sales contracts, buyer invoices, dividend runs, compliance dashboard, annual report, business plan builder, offline entry

### Phase 3
Processing, traceability and export support, loan and insurance packs, credit profiles, USSD/IVR, ABC benchmarking
