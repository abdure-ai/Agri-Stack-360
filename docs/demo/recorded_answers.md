## [en] How much wheat seed do I need for one hectare?

- kind: `answer` · confidence: `high` · escalated: `False` · flags: `-`

> For midland areas, the required seed amount depends on your planting method and soil condition [S5]. If you broadcast seed on well-prepared fertile, loamy, or clay soil, you need 150 kg per hectare [S5]. If you broadcast on poorly prepared, sandy, or acidic soil, you need 175 kg per hectare [S5]. If you sow in rows on well-prepared fertile, loamy, or clay soil, you need 125 kg per hectare [S5]. For row planting on light and red soils, you need 125–150 kg per hectare [S3]. For row planting on black soil, use 150–175 kg per hectare [S3].

- S5: MoA Irrigated Wheat Production Package, 2016 E.C., pp. 13–14
- S3: MoA Bread and Durum Wheat Production Manual, 2016 E.C., p. 19

## [en] How often should I irrigate wheat?

- kind: `answer` · confidence: `high` · escalated: `False` · flags: `-`

> Irrigation timing depends on your soil type, crop stage, and local climate [S4]. For your wheat at the tillering stage, irrigate every 7 to 10 days for loamy sand soil, 8 to 10 days for loam soil, or 10 to 12 days for clay soil [C]. In midland areas, wheat requires a total net irrigation of 350 to 500 mm throughout the growing season [S2]. Do not allow water shortage during key growth stages such as seedling, flowering, and grain filling [S4]. Stop applying irrigation water two weeks before harvesting [S4].

- S4: MoA Irrigated Wheat Production Manual, 2016 E.C., p. 22
